Your CRM assistant, explained simply

What happens between your question and its answer

Every time someone asks the assistant something — "where's my order?", "what's our refund policy?" — the question quietly passes through a series of checkpoints before an answer comes back. Here's that journey, step by step.

💬

Someone asks a question

A customer or teammate types something into the assistant, in their own words — no special format needed.

"What's the status of order #4521?"
🛡️

A quick safety check

Before anything else happens, the question is checked to make sure it's appropriate and actually answerable — filtering out anything unsafe or off-topic.

🧭

Deciding who should answer

The system reads the question and figures out which specialist is best suited to handle it — or if both are needed.

Numbers expert

Looks things up in the database

Order status, revenue, account history — anything that lives in structured records.

Knowledge expert

Searches documents and policies

FAQs, policy pages, guides — anything written down in company documents.

🧩

Combining what was found

If both experts contributed, their findings are merged into a single, coherent response — no duplicate or conflicting information.

✅

You get a plain-language answer

Delivered back in everyday language, ready to read — the whole trip usually takes a fraction of a second.

Quietly working in the background

These don't show up in the conversation, but they're what keep every answer accurate, fast, and safe.

Smart routing

Always sends the question to the right expert, every time.

Thorough search

Looks through documents in more than one way, so nothing relevant is missed.

Reranking

Re-checks what was found and puts the most relevant results first.

Shared connections

A common language that lets tools and experts pass information to each other.

Guardrails

Keeps the assistant from answering unsafe, unrelated, or inappropriate requests.

Activity logging

Keeps a record of what happened, so issues can be traced and fixed quickly.

Regular testing

Answers are checked against known-good examples to catch mistakes early.

Memory cache

Remembers recent answers so repeat questions come back almost instantly.

# Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** August 18, 2026

## 1. Overview

We want our customers to have a positive experience with our products and services. This Refund Policy explains when customers may request a refund, how refunds are processed, and any applicable conditions.

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds

Customers may request a refund when:

- The product is defective or damaged upon delivery.
- The wrong product was delivered.
- The product received does not match the product description.
- The order was cancelled before shipment.
- A service was not delivered as promised.

Refund eligibility may depend on the product category and the specific terms presented at the time of purchase.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items

The following may not be eligible for a refund:

- Products damaged due to customer misuse.
- Products returned after the applicable return period.
- Customized or personalized products, unless defective.
- Digital products that have already been downloaded or consumed.
- Products missing essential accessories or original packaging, where required.

## 4. Refund Request

To request a refund, customers should provide:

- Order number.
- Reason for the refund.
- Photos or other evidence when the product is damaged or defective.
- Any other information requested by customer support.

## 5. Refund Processing

Once the returned product is received and inspected, the refund request will be reviewed.

If approved, the refund will normally be issued to the original payment method.

Processing time may vary depending on the payment provider or bank.

## 6. Damaged or Incorrect Products

Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product.

Where applicable, we may offer:

- Replacement.
- Full refund.
- Partial refund.
- Store credit.

## 7. Contact Support

For refund-related questions, customers should contact customer support with their order number and relevant details.

## 8. Policy Changes

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The latest version published on this page will apply to future purchases.
